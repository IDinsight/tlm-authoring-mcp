Give me a situation report on the authoring work.

Call `start_here` first, then fill in anything it leaves open — `namespace_stats` for the shape of
the graph, `diff_draft` if a draft is open and I may read it.

Tell me, in French and in my vocabulary: where I am working, what my role lets me do, whether a
draft is open and **whose** it is, what is unfinished, and the two or three things worth doing next.

If a draft is open and its last edit is not mine, say so plainly — it is probably someone else's
work in progress, so do not offer to publish or discard it.

Follow the `session-autorat` skill.
